# Third-party material

## Release notes of pandas and NumPy

The following files are copies of, or quote items of, the official release notes of pandas and NumPy and
the NumPy 2.0 migration guide:

- `screening/release_notes/`: copies taken at the release tags of pandas-dev/pandas and numpy/numpy
  (see `screening/README.md`);
- `measurements/data/pandas_v2.0.0_whatsnew.rst`: a copy of `doc/source/whatsnew/v2.0.0.rst` of pandas;
- files that quote these items, such as `screening/results/release_note_entries.csv`,
  `screening/results/release_note_decisions.csv` and `conditions/results/` (the documentation conditions),
  and the prompts and replies that contain documentation.

These texts remain under the BSD 3-Clause licenses of their projects, reproduced in
`third_party_licenses/pandas-LICENSE` (pandas, tag `v2.2.0`) and `third_party_licenses/numpy-LICENSE.txt`
(NumPy, tag `v2.1.0`).

## CPython

`levenshtein` in `conditions/error_variants.py` is a Python translation of the distance measure of the
interpreter's "Did you mean" suggestions (`Python/suggestions.c` of CPython 3.11). CPython is
Copyright (c) 2001 Python Software Foundation; All Rights Reserved, and is licensed under the PSF License
Agreement, reproduced in `third_party_licenses/cpython-LICENSE` (tag `v3.11.0`).

## Replies of the models

`runs/results/`, `probe/results/` and `synthetic/results/` contain replies generated by GPT-6 Luna (OpenAI),
Gemini 3.8 Flash (Google), Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct (Alibaba Cloud) and CodeLlama-7B-Instruct (Meta). They
are included so that the results can be recomputed; their use may also be subject to the terms of the
providers of these models.
